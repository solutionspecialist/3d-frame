Open the 3MF in your slicer; the STL is the same part for other tools.

Slice it yourself. Keep the orientation it loads in: card slot on top, frame width along the bed's front-to-back axis. Set the 75% overhang speed and the external bridge speed to 10 mm/s: the window's top edge prints as an overhanging wall. Brim on, supports off.

Defaults are a business card and 8 × 1.75 mm magnets in PETG, measured on a Sovol SV06 Plus; fits and the 46 mm bridge are proven there, not on other printers. Leave Border, Motif scale and the fan counts empty for automatic. Source and tests: github.com/solutionspecialist/3d-frame. Public domain (CC0): use it for anything.
